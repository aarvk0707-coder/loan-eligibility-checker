AI Financial Tips

Practical starter tips to build healthy money habits.

📊

Keep EMIs manageable

Try to keep all monthly loan payments within a comfortable share of your take-home income.

⏰

Pay on time

Set reminders or auto-pay for bills and loan payments to avoid missed due dates.

🧾

Check your report

Review your credit report periodically and dispute information that looks incorrect.

🛟

Build an emergency fund

Save gradually for unexpected expenses before taking on new monthly commitments.

🔍

Compare loan offers

Compare the annual rate, fees, tenure, prepayment rules, and total repayment—not just the EMI.

🔐

Protect your data

Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords, or sensitive banking details with unknown people.
